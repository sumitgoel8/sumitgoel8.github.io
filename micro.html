Microeconomics

This course introduces the major concepts and tools of microeconomics. It studies the behavior of fundamental economic agents and uses this to evaluate institutions through which scarce resources are allocated. Much of the course focuses on the market mechanism, its efficiency, and its failures. The course concludes with an introduction to game theory.
