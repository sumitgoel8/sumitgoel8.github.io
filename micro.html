Microeconomics

This course is an introduction to microeconomics. Broadly, microeconomics offers a framework for studying how institutions shape the utilization of scarce resources through the behavior they induce among fundamental economic agents. We begin by discussing why designing good institutions is fundamentally challenging. Much of the course then focuses on the market mechanism, its efficiency, and its failures. It concludes with an introduction to game theory.
